# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Tidied the power-button reboot menu: the button keeps a soft circular highlight while the menu is open, the menu drops from the button with its right edge aligned to it, rounded corners and even padding. Options unchanged, no icons in the menu.
- Redesigned the bottom navigation bar: cleaner floating pill with no border, soft shadow, fixed 48dp tabs, and a selected tab that expands smoothly to show its label.
- New navigation icons: Home, wrench for Flash, box for Backups, and Settings.
- Softened the active tab highlight: Medium-weight label and a subtler selected pill in dark and light themes.
- Language settings now offer English only, and English is the default. Removed Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan) and Traditional Chinese (Hong Kong).
